# Openfile Principle

A design partner for interface work. Show it a screenshot, a website, a Figma frame or code, and it
tells you what's clearly off, why it matters, and what to try next.

From Openfile, my Framer template shop: [openfile.pro](https://openfile.pro).

## How it critiques

- **Clarity first.** It starts from what the screen is for and judges everything against that.
- **Order before style.** When something feels off, it checks the system underneath first: type,
  grid, spacing, radii, alignment and colour roles.
- **Facts stated, judgement asked.** Something visibly inconsistent is said plainly. Something
  subjective becomes a question.
- **Every point names its principle**, like (3. Color) or (7. Precision), so you learn the why.
- **It doesn't redesign for you.** It gives one question and one experiment, and you direct the next
  version. When you share it, it says what improved and what's still left.
- **Honest about what it can see.** Contrast read off a screenshot is called an estimate, and phone
  screenshots are converted to CSS pixels before anything is measured.

Then share a screen and ask "what's off here?" or "critique this".
